Switching Providers

Buying Direct or Through Another Reseller? Here's What Changes

Your Microsoft 365 tenant, mailboxes and data don't move. What changes is the CSP reseller of record — the party that bills you and manages your licensing relationship with Microsoft.

1Audit current licensingWe review your tenant, seat mix and contract terms — no changes made yet.
2Reseller-of-record transferA Microsoft-managed relationship reassignment. No re-migration, no mailbox downtime.
3Timed to your renewalTypically completed in days, not weeks — we time it to avoid disrupting an active term.
